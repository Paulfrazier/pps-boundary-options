## What this says

- **Sabin elevator.** The state record dates Sabin's passenger elevator to a final inspection on April 17, 1995, which fits the 1995 bond that "install[ed] elevators at 15 schools" (pps-2013-ada-executive-summary.md). PPS's 2021 Long-Range Facility Plan says "An elevator was installed in the facility in 2007" (lrfp-2021-vol2-sabin.md). The 2007 date may refer to later work. The state record also shows a 2011 controller and door alteration.
- **Sabin lift.** Only one wheelchair lift is registered at Sabin: a portable Ascension lift serving the theater stage, with 3.2 ft of travel, installed 2023-24. That matches the 2020 bond's main-level scope, "Installation of lifts at theater stages" (pps-bond-accessibility-and-ada.md).

## Search scope caveat

The public search box matches record numbers, addresses and project descriptions. Old or oddly addressed equipment can be missed, so a negative result is strong evidence but not proof.
