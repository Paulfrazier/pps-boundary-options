# Oregon elevator program database: Irvington searches

Portal: https://aca-oregon.accela.com/SWS_PROD/ (linked as "Elevator Permits" from Oregon ePermitting; ElevatorPermits.Oregon.gov). Searched 2026-10-26 with the global search box.

## Address note
One address in circulation for Irvington is 1888 NE Brazee St. Every PPS source in this repo gives **1320 NE Brazee St**: the 2025-26 school profile ("Address: 1320 NE BRAZEE ST", "NE 14th Ave & NE Brazee St"), the school website (irvington.pps.net), and the 2009 PPS reports. Both addresses were searched.

## Reading
If Irvington had an elevator or a registered platform lift, a record would be expected at its street address, as Sabin's equipment is. Its absence is strong evidence that Irvington has neither. It is not proof: the search matches text, and equipment filed under an unusual address or description could be missed.
