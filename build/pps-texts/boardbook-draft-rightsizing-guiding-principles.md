 RESOLUTION No. # 
 
Resolution Adopting Guiding Principles and Workplan in the Rightsizing Process for Portland Public Schools 
 
RECITALS 
  
A.​ Whereas Portland Public Schools (PPS) is committed to well-resourced sustainable schools so that every PPS 
student has equitable access to a robust and high-quality educational experience;  
 
B.​ Whereas the Student Assignment to Neighborhood Schools Policy 4.10.045-P directs the Superintendent to 
regularly monitor enrollment, program demand and demographic trends; 
 
C.​ Whereas PPS enrollment has declined steadily for the past five years and is forecast to continue to decline for at 
least the next nine years. At the same time, our facilities are aging and costs have increased, forcing resource 
strains across schools;   
 
D.​ Whereas on April 14, 2026,  the Board of Directors adopted Resolution 7296 directing the Superintendent to 
launch the Growing Great Schools districtwide rightsizing process to consider school and program consolidations, 
boundary adjustment, special program changes, and grade reconfigurations; 
 
E.​ Whereas the Board finds it desirable to adopt Guiding Principles to ensure that the districtwide rightsizing process 
results in well-resourced, sustainable schools and equitable access for all students; and 
 
F.​ Whereas the Board finds it desirable to adopt a Workplan for the districtwide rightsizing process that ensures that 
students, families, and community, as well as financial transparency and stewardship of district resources, are at 
the forefront of rightsizing decisions. 
 
RESOLUTION 
 
1.  Now, therefore, the Board of Directors hereby adopts the districtwide rightsizing Guiding Principles and Workplan, 
attached as Exhibit 1 hereto.  
 
2.  The Board directs the Superintendent to oversee and direct the districtwide rightsizing process in accordance with the 
Guiding Principles and Workplan.

Exhibit 1 Guiding Principles and Workplan: 
 
Guiding Principles 
The following Guiding Principles will establish priorities and guide decision-making at all stages of the rightsizing process:  
a.​ Well-Resourced Schools. Students across Portland Public Schools will attend well-resourced sustainable 
schools  
i.​
PPS will provide comparable, sustainable and rich academic programming at all grade spans 
across the district so that students can attend affirming learning spaces that meet their needs and 
that are buffered from enrollment fluctuations.   
ii.​
Descriptions of well resourced schools by grade spans will be drafted and shared with the Board 
and community and will serve as the aspirational vision that will  guide rightsizing decisions. 
b.​ Healthy Learning Environments. PPS will provide safe, welcoming, and healthy learning environments 
that are conducive to student success and well-being. We will seek to responsibly steward our resources 
and maintain a sustainable portfolio of school facilities that support academic programming and long-term 
operational viability.  
c.​ Educational Options. PPS operates a variety of schools and programs, all of which will be part of this 
rightsizing process, with neighborhood schools at the center of the work.  
i.​
Access to choice programs will be balanced with the goal of assuring well-resourced 
neighborhood schools across our system. 
d.​ PPS Equity Lens. PPS will apply the Equity Lens to inform decision-making which will consider student 
demographics, socioeconomic status, and the needs of special education students and multilingual 
learners.  
 
 
Workplan  
a.​ Approach and Analysis  
i.​
PPS will conduct a comprehensive, data-informed analysis to develop a district-wide rightsizing 
plan that is designed to support equitable access to well-resourced, academically robust, and 
fiscally sustainable schools and programs. The analysis will incorporate multiple data sources, 
including, but not limited to, enrollment trends and projections, program access and parity, 
student demographics, facility utilization, and financial conditions.  
ii.​
PPS will be transparent about the data sources used in its analysis.  
iii.​
In parallel, the District will assess the operational feasibility of potential scenarios, including 
staffing, transportation, facilities, nutrition services, program continuity, and financial impacts, to 
confirm that proposed strategies are viable and sustainable. 
iv.​
Each proposed scenario shall include a standardized equity impact analysis that: 
1. Assesses the impacts on focal student groups using disaggregated data; 
2. Assesses access to specialized programs, including choice schools or programs, and 
culturally relevant supports, transportation, and proximity 
3. Assesses impacts on school communities that have recently been impacted by recent 
enrollment and program changes as well as Title I school communities. 
4. Incorporates community input and reflects how feedback informed the 
recommendation.  
 
b.​ Community Engagement 
i.​
PPS will engage the broader community in a structured way to get feedback in multiple phases 
including guiding principles and scenarios, and the plan for engagement will be shared publicly. 
ii.​
PPS will intentionally engage with families and educators to receive feedback from a variety of 
perspectives including from historically underserved communities, communities that have recently 
been impacted by enrollment and program balancing decisions and those who will be most 
impacted by decisions in this process.

c.​ Implementation Plan 
i.​
PPS staff will develop an implementation framework to operationalize the rightsizing plan over 
time in a manner that minimizes disruption for students, families, and staff, and supports effective 
coordination across central office functions. Implementation will be sequenced based on district 
priorities, urgency of need, and operational readiness. 
ii.​
The implementation plan will establish key timelines, dependencies, and decision points, 
including alignment with staffing, budget, and facility planning cycles. It will also identify required 
resources for students, families, and staff to support continuity of programming and successful 
transitions for impacted school communities.  
iii.​
Consistent with PPS’ commitment to continuous improvement, implementation will include 
monitoring, evaluation, and adjustment based on outcomes and lessons learned. PPS will 
continue to engage students, families, staff, and community partners throughout implementation 
to inform refinements and support equitable outcomes. 
iv.​
Staff will present the proposed implementation plan to the Board for final approval.
