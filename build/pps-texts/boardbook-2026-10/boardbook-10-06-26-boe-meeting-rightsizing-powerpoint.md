## Page 10

Applying Criteria to Develop Scenarios 
Guiding Question: How can we increase the number of school sites that 
operate in a sustainable enrollment range, so that students across our district can 
experience stable and adequately resourced programs while also using our 
buildings eﬃciently?
Deﬁnition Sustainable Enrollment Range:
 Minimum enrollment to offer 
stable programming.
Threshold: minimum enrollment
●Elementary threshold 360 students
●K8 and Middle School threshold 500 students
●High School threshold 1100 students
Functional capacity:  A practical estimate of how many students a school site 
accommodates.

## Page 11

Applying Criteria to Develop Scenarios 
●Enrollment Trends & Capacity:
 Evaluated projected enrollment 
patterns and capacity by grade band through 2031-32.   
●Facility & Capital Viability: 
Assessed facility condition alongside 
enrollment to identify viability of site. 
●Programmatic & Grade-Band Optimization:
 Examined K-8 to 
K-5/6-8 conversions and specialized programs during regional 
analyses to strengthen middle school cohorts and DLI pathways as 
well as feeder pattern alignment where possible.
●Student & Operational Guardrails:
 Capped receiving schools at 
no more than two per closure. Ensured students transition to 
higher-quality facilities.
