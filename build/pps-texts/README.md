# PPS Rightsizing Scenarios: Plain-Text Archive

Retrieved: 2026-10-12

Unofficial text extraction of a public PPS document for accessibility and research. May contain extraction errors; consult the original at <source_url>.

Source start page: https://www.pps.net/about/portland-public-schools-information/rightsize/scenarios

Documents archived: 122 Markdown files (10 pps.net web pages including the start page, and 112 PDFs: 1 scenarios overview, 4 regional summaries, 82 school Family Guides, 6 feeder-pattern translations, 2 SEGC reports, 1 MOU, 15 BoardBook documents, 1 Google Slides export). Originals (untouched downloads, renamed only to .pdf/.html) are in `originals/`. Each .md has YAML front matter with source URL, parent link, sha256 of the original, page count and extraction method.

Method notes: PDFs extracted with PyMuPDF (content order; columns stay grouped). For PDFs of 20 pages or fewer, a second per-page "row-wise reading" (pdfplumber) is appended so table rows read on one line; where the two renderings differ, the original PDF is authoritative. No OCR was run, so text inside images is not captured. Maps/charts: only text labels are captured; boundary lines, shading and imagery are not. Web pages were converted from the raw HTML; the school list on the 'How Could Your School Be Impacted?' page is loaded by a JavaScript iframe and was captured with a headless browser.

### family-guide-cesar-chavez.md, page 3
> CÉSAR CHÁVEZ School proposal | Options A and B ... Complex school setup: Hosting multiple complex programs (DLI, neighborhood K-8, special education) on one school created unbalanced class sizes and operational issues. Focused language focus: Moving to a single-focus K-5 DLI school creates a regional Spanish language program location. ... This is a proposal, not a final decision. What is being proposed? César Chávez would change from a K-8 to a focused K-5 regional Spanish DLI school. It would welcome Spanish DLI students from Beach, Sitton, and James John. Neighborhood (non-DLI) K-5 students would move to Rosa Parks K-5. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to George Middle School. ... Regional domino effect: Changing César Chávez opens up the necessary space at Sitton and Chief Joseph to welcome neighborhood students from closing schools.
>
> Snapshot: Current program "K-8 neighborhood/language immersion"; Expected enrollment with no changes "413 in 2027-28; 392 in 2031-32"; District school size goal "More than 500 students"; Students living within 1 mile "82.8%".
