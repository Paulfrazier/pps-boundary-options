# equity_noswap_alameda: trade-offs (model v2, PPS horizon)

Generated by `build/solver/report_pps2031.py` from `build/solver/out_v2/{status_quo,scenario_A,equity_noswap_alameda}/scorecard.json` and `schools.csv`. Model v2 (`build/solver/METHOD.md` §6, §7; `out_v2/CALIBRATION.md`). This plan uses Scenario A's buildings (its 14 closures) and 9-12 lines, but not all of its immersion moves: Atkinson Spanish, Scott Spanish, MLK Jr Mandarin and Beaumont Spanish (6-8) stay home, and Lent, Rigler and Cesar Chavez are neighborhood K-5 schools that keep their Spanish programs. PPS Status Quo and PPS Scenario A are shown for reference. All plans are scored on the same horizon: **required** = meet or beat Scenario A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32 (PPS Family Guide p. 1: "Estimates cover 2027-28 through 2031-32" [PPS]); 2032-33 → 2035-36 **reported only** (last year of PSU's per-school Table 5.5 [PPS/PSU]); nothing past 2035-36. "At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**Why this run is published.** It is build step 2 of the current best ("Current best: fewer DLI moves, sustainable schools", `equity_noswap_alameda_rig_hs`), published so that plan can be re-run from this repository alone. It is not a plan shown on the site.

**What it is.** Step 1's lines (`equity_noswap`) with big-school relief (an elementary school over 450 kids gets a soft ceiling of 450, and moving kids off it costs less), with at most 300 elementary kids allowed to move off step 1's elementary lines. Middle and high-school lines are step 1's, unchanged. Step 3 (`equity_noswap_alameda_rig`) starts from these elementary lines and adds the Rigler-area correction; the current best adds the Skyline high-school fix.
