**Rules missed.**
- **Meets or beats Scenario A** in every required year at every level.
- **But three schools are out of range in 2031-32:** Forest Park −101 (K-5), Jackson −78 and Roseway Heights −35 (6-8).
  - **Forest Park.** PPS A has the same shortfall (−102). Within 2 miles' walk there aren't enough K-5 kids around its building, so this looks structural under the cap [inferred: our model; near-proven, K-5 gap 0.16%].
  - **Jackson and Roseway Heights.** These are left by the whole-zone middle plan, which is proven optimal at the zone level. The split search ran three times: once from the old relaxation hint, once with the phase-2 warm start plus repair_hint, and once capped at 6 splits with 1,200 s. It never found a plan with less shortfall. Capped at 6 splits it ended with Jackson −63, Tubman −94, West Sylvan −10, which is worse, so it was rejected.
  - Jackson's and Forest Park's shortfalls are also in PPS A. Allowing more elementary-zone splits at 6-8 might close them; that wasn't run.
- **"Fewest" still moves more kids than PPS A.**
  - K-5: 3,594 kids reassigned vs PPS Status Quo (A 2,935).
  - 6-8: 2,762 (A 1,972).
  - Meeting the size and durability rules forces moves A doesn't make, because A leaves 20 schools out of range.
  - Against "Redrawn walkable K-8 lines, PPS high schools" (`option1_durable`), it moves 1,281 fewer K-5 kids, and K-5 walks only slightly longer (0.969 vs 0.953 mi).

**Edge case handled.** One small block on the Columbia River edge has no school within 2.0 miles' walk. It gets its nearest-walk schools and its neighbours' nearest in-cap school as candidates (`max_walk_fallback_nearest`). No other block is affected.
