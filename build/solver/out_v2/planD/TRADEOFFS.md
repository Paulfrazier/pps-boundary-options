All plans are scored on the same horizon: **required** = meet or beat Scenario A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32 (PPS Family Guide p. 1: "Estimates cover 2027-28 through 2031-32" [PPS]); 2032-33 → 2035-36 **reported only** (last year of PSU's per-school Table 5.5 [PPS/PSU]); nothing past 2035-36. "At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**What this plan is (request, Plan D as written).** Plan D is a board member's alternative to PPS A and B, as reported by The Oregonian on Oct 20, 2026 (the memo itself is not public; summary in `build/pps-texts/oregonian-2026-10-20-splitt-scenario-d.md`) [second-hand: newspaper report of the memo]. It closes six elementary schools: Creston (to Arleta), Buckman, Woodmere, Sabin (to Vernon and Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.), Irvington and Peninsula. Immersion programs stay in their current buildings. The model keeps PPS Status Quo lines, grade setups and programs and sends each closing school's area to its receiver; Sabin (MLK Jr 122 / Vernon 114 K-5 kids in 2031-32) and Woodmere (Whitman 193 / Lewis 31) are split by shortest walk. The article names receivers only for Creston, Sabin and part of Woodmere, so Buckman (Abernethy south of Burnside / Sunnyside north), Irvington (Beverly Cleary) and Peninsula (Rosa Parks) use PPS A's receivers and the rest of Woodmere goes to Whitman [assumed]. Creston's deaf and hard of hearing program is not modeled. Lines are not solved; the plan is scored like the PPS baselines (`build/solver/build_planD.py`).

**What it shows.** Six closures reassign 1,223 K-5 kids (PPS A 2,935) and move no immersion student (PPS A 1,567; `out_v2/POVERTY_COMPARE.md`). Most elementary schools stay small: 13 of Plan D's 44 counted K-5 schools are at target in 2031-32 (and 13 every year 2027-28 to 2031-32), against PPS Status Quo 9 of 50 and PPS A 21 of 35 [inferred: our model on PSU July 2026 Table 5.5]. Merging whole areas without redrawing them sends 2,230 K-5 kids past a closer school (Status Quo 1,861; PPS A 1,970). Vernon reaches 605 in 2027-28, and Beverly Cleary (+86), Sunnyside (+108) and Vernon (+14) are over capacity in 2031-32.

**Correction (2026-10-22).** The first scoring of this plan (2026-10-21) kept the six closed buildings in the K-5 school list, so they counted as open (K-5 `schools_open` 54; 48 is right: 46 zoned + 2 program-only), as non-exempt schools below target (denominator 50; 44 is right), and as "a closer school" for kids living near them. Past a closer school was 2,621; it is 2,230 with the closed buildings removed. Lines, sizes and at-target counts are unchanged.

**Assignment pieces (exclave check exceptions).** `check_exclaves.py` finds cut-off pieces, all of them PPS Status Quo's own (the same list as `out_v2/status_quo`; this plan does not redraw lines): K-5 Faubion 73 blocks / 198 kids (2031-32) and 4 blocks / 5 kids, Skyline 7 blocks / 3.5 kids and 1 block / 0.6, Chapman 1 and 2 blocks (under 1 kid), Beach 2 blocks (0.3); 6-8 George 60 blocks / 80 kids, Faubion 73 / 123 and 4 / 3, Skyline 7 / 1.0 and 1 / 0.3, Ockley Green 1 block (0.2); 9-12 McDaniel 362 blocks / 322 kids, Jefferson 4 / 2.0 and 2 / 0.1.
