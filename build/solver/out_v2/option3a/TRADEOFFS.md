# Close 2 focus options (Winterhaven, da Vinci): trade-offs (model v2, PPS horizon)

Generated by `build/solver/report_pps2031.py` from `build/solver/out_v2/{scenario_A,option1_durable_msfeed_off,option3a}/scorecard.json` and `schools.csv`. Model v2 (`build/solver/METHOD.md` §6, §7; `out_v2/CALIBRATION.md`). All plans here use Scenario A's schools and programs and 9-12 lines and are scored on the same horizon: **required** = meet or beat Scenario A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32 (PPS Family Guide p. 1: "Estimates cover 2027-28 through 2031-32" [PPS]); 2032-33 → 2035-36 **reported only** (last year of PSU's per-school Table 5.5 [PPS/PSU]); nothing past 2035-36. "At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers]. The walkable baseline is the earlier v2 solve with no feeder rules and no nesting; its lines were solved for the old 2031-32 → 2040-41 window and are re-scored here.

**What this plan is.** PPS Scenario A's closures, programs and 9-12 lines, plus two focus options close: **Winterhaven** (K-8, STEM) and **da Vinci** (6-8, arts). Their students return to their neighborhood schools, and every elementary and middle line is redrawn under the same rules as "Redrawn K-8, PPS HS" (`option1_durable`): at least PPS A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32, walkable K-5, a soft middle→high feeder cost, PPS A's high-school lines. Benson, MLC, Odyssey and ACCESS stay open.

**Where returning students live.** Placed by catchment from a per-catchment spreadsheet of 2025-26 focus-option students (`build/pps-texts/focus-students-by-catchment-sheet-2025-26-public.md`). That file is **second-hand**: its first columns match PPS's Enrollment Summary Oct 2025 row for row, but the per-focus-school columns are not in that PDF and their original source is not stated.
- Its rows sum exactly to the 2025-26 enrollments we use (da Vinci 429, Winterhaven 316).
- In-district students: da Vinci 423, Winterhaven 301. The 21 who live outside PPS are not returned to any school.
- Elementary rows = K-5 residents and middle rows = 6-8. K-8 rows are split by Winterhaven's grade mix. Within a catchment, students are spread by Census residents of that age.
- The sheet concentrates them near their schools, unlike the district-wide estimate used before. Winterhaven, grades 6-8 by middle-school catchment: Hosford 45, Sellwood 26, Lane 18 (its K-5 students sit in the elementary rows). da Vinci: Ockley Green 60, Tubman 56, Roseway Heights 40 (sheet rows, 2025-26).
- **Assumption:** every returning student attends their neighborhood school (none leave PPS or pick another option). Sizes in 2031-32 follow PSU's Table 5.5 series for each program.

**Not modeled:** what happens to the Winterhaven and da Vinci buildings.
