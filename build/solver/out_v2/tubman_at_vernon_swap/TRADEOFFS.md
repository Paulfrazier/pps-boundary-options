"At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**What this plan is (request).** Harriet Tubman Middle School moves out of its building on N Flint Ave, next to I-5, into the Vernon building on NE Killingsworth. Vernon's elementary grades close, and its families go to nearby elementaries that feed the Vernon building or Beaumont. Sabin stays open, every Sabin-area family stays at Sabin, and all of Irvington goes to Beverly Cleary together. Middle schools by elementary: Sabin, King and Faubion's main neighborhood go to Tubman in the Vernon building. Boise-Eliot/Humboldt goes to Ockley Green, which is closer than the Vernon building for most of its families. Beverly Cleary and Alameda go to Beaumont. Faubion's far north blocks go to Ockley Green, and its Bybee Lake shelter area goes to Astor, as Plan D says. High-school lines are the base plan's, with Irvington teens at Grant. The move is modeled as of 2029-30: the solver had no per-year capacity, so the Vernon building's seats were set to 640 as a stand-in, and the sizes below are checked against its real 552 (PPS School Profiles 2025-26). Built from `tubman_at_vernon_irvbc` (elementary lines solved), then `tubman_at_vernon_irvbc_ms` (middle lines re-solved with the feeders above). The Boise-Eliot/Faubion middle-school swap was then set by `build_tubvern_swap.py`, not solved.

**What it shows.** The Tubman middle school in the Vernon building has 574, 549, 532, 519 and 524 students in 2027-28 through 2031-32. It is over its 552 seats only in 2027-28, which is why the move is phased: portables or a flex year cover the peak. Ockley Green has 530, 494, 523, 516 and 526 (one year under 500), and Beaumont falls from 701 to 662 (737 seats). In 2031-32, NE middle schoolers (Vernon building, Beaumont, Ockley Green, Roseway Heights) walk 1.29 mi on average, with 513 over the 1.5-mile no-bus distance; under PPS A it is 1.63 mi and 824. Kids sent past a closer NE middle school fall from 493 (PPS A) to 109, and the Tubman building sits near the middle of its students instead of at the edge (`build/strategy/tubman_vernon/site_data/ms_compactness.json`, `ne_ms_totals.json`). Elementary schools in 2031-32: Sabin 420, Beverly Cleary 476, King 407, Alameda 580, Boise-Eliot/Humboldt 377. Costs: about 288 Vernon K-5 students change schools, and the Vernon building (70,833 sq ft, no passenger elevator) needs work to serve as a middle school.

**Assignment pieces.** `check_exclaves.py`: none at any level.
