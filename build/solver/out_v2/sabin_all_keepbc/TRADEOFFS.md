All plans here use Scenario A's schools and programs and 9-12 lines and are scored on the same horizon: **required** = meet or beat Scenario A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32 (PPS Family Guide p. 1: "Estimates cover 2027-28 through 2031-32" [PPS]); 2032-33 → 2035-36 **reported only** (last year of PSU's per-school Table 5.5 [PPS/PSU]); nothing past 2035-36. "At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**What this plan is.** "Sabin into Irvington" (Sabin closes, Irvington stays open; PPS A closes both) with one more rule: today's Beverly Cleary area stays whole with Beverly Cleary, so Irvington can't grow by taking Beverly Cleary families. As in "Sabin into Irvington": built on the current best ("Current best: fewer DLI moves, sustainable schools"; at most 1,200 elementary kids moved off its lines), MLK Jr keeps Mandarin, today's King, Boise-Eliot/Humboldt and Vernon areas stay with their own schools, and Irvington may stay below 360 (exempt from the floor) rather than pull kids from its neighbors. Kids zoned to Irvington go to Tubman, then Jefferson; kids zoned to Alameda or Beverly Cleary go to Beaumont, then Grant. Gray middle school may run short of 500 (not counted) rather than the middle lines swapping zones to fill it.

**Result in brief.** Nearly the same as "Sabin into Irvington": Irvington 224 elementary students in 2031-32 (below 360, exempt), Beverly Cleary 361, Alameda 382, Vernon 364, MLK Jr 445; every elementary school at target every year (38 of 38); Middle schools at target 11, 11, 11, 11, 11 of 12 counted by year (Gray may run short of 500 and is not counted: 447 in 2031-32; George outside its range, Roseway Heights outside its range); Beaumont 607, Tubman 562. One clean-up after the solve: two blocks (about 2 high-school students) that the Irvington-to-Jefferson / Beverly Cleary-to-Grant rule cut off from Cleveland were given to Jefferson, the school around them.
