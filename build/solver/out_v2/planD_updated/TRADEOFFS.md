All plans are scored on the same horizon: **required** = meet or beat Scenario A's count of schools at target every year 2027-28 → 2031-32 (PPS Family Guide p. 1: "Estimates cover 2027-28 through 2031-32" [PPS]); 2032-33 → 2035-36 **reported only** (last year of PSU's per-school Table 5.5 [PPS/PSU]); nothing past 2035-36. "At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**What this plan is (request, Plan D as written, updated).** Plan D is a board member's draft framework, offered as a compromise between PPS A/B and fewer changes; it is a public Google Doc revised since its first posting on 10/23/26, and PPS staff have not reviewed it (`build/pps-texts/scenario-d-draft-framework-2026-10-25.md`; re-checked 10/27: wording changes only, no change to closures, receivers or feeders) [primary: the draft itself]. It replaces the earlier Plan D built from the Oregonian's 10/20 account (`out_v2/planD`), in which Sabin closed into Vernon and King; in the draft Sabin stays open. It closes five elementary schools: Creston (to Arleta), Woodmere (to Whitman, part of Whitman's area to Lewis as in Scenario B), Peninsula and Cesar Chavez's neighborhood program (to Rosa Parks), Irvington (to Beverly Cleary) and Buckman (to Abernethy and Sunnyside, Scenario A's lines). Beverly Cleary, Laurelhurst, Sunnyside, Bridger, Faubion and Vernon become K-5s; their middle schoolers go to Beaumont, Mt Tabor, Hosford, Harrison Park, Ockley Green and Tubman. Creston-area graduates go to Kellogg, Maplewood to Gray, Lewis to Lane, Irvington to Beaumont and Peninsula to George; Beaumont's Spanish program moves to Roseway Heights. The model keeps Status Quo lines everywhere else; lines are not solved, and the plan is scored like the PPS baselines (`build/solver/build_planD_updated.py`). Not modeled: Odyssey into the MLC building and MLC ending grades 9-12, Creston's deaf and hard of hearing and Woodmere's classrooms, the Bybee Lake shelter area moving from Faubion to Astor, Skyline graduates choosing a high school, and the phasing (current Irvington 5th graders at Tubman, Beaumont DLI 6th and 7th graders staying through 2028-29; the end state is modeled).

**What it shows.** The closures reassign 1,155 K-5 kids off Status Quo (PPS A 2,935). In 2031-32, 12 of 47 counted K-5 schools are at target (14 in 2027-28), against PPS Status Quo 9 and PPS A 21; 7 middle schools are at target in 2031-32 (Status Quo 3, A 7) [inferred: our model on PSU July 2026 Table 5.5]. Merging whole areas without redrawing them sends 2,153 K-5 kids past a closer school (Status Quo 1,861; PPS A 1,970). No school is over capacity in 2031-32. Receivers in 2031-32: Beverly Cleary 451, Rosa Parks 464, Arleta 402, Sunnyside 411, Abernethy 355, Whitman 339, Lewis 308; Sabin stays at 236 and Vernon at 288. Middle schools: Beaumont 464, Roseway Heights 731, Tubman 423.

**Assignment pieces (exclave check exceptions).** `check_exclaves.py` finds cut-off pieces, almost all PPS Status Quo's own (this plan does not redraw lines): K-5 Faubion 73 blocks / 198 kids (2031-32) and 4 blocks / 4.7, Skyline 7 / 3.5 and 1 / 0.6, Chapman 1 and 2 blocks (under 1 kid), Beach 2 blocks (0.3); 6-8 Ockley Green 4 blocks / 3.1 (Faubion's Bybee Lake piece, now via Faubion's middle-school move) and 1 / 0.6 and 1 / 0.2, Hosford 2 blocks / 0.9 and 1 / 0.4 (Sunnyside's area, now feeding Hosford), Skyline 7 / 1.0 and 1 / 0.3; 9-12 McDaniel 362 blocks / 322 kids, Jefferson 4 / 2.0 and 2 / 0.1.
