"At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers].

**What this plan is (request, Plan D with every school held to 360).** Plan D's buildings and programs (see `out_v2/planD/TRADEOFFS.md`: a board member's alternative as reported by The Oregonian on Oct 20, 2026; summary in `build/pps-texts/oregonian-2026-10-20-splitt-scenario-d.md`; no immersion moves). Question: with only these six closures, can every elementary school reach PPS's 360 target if the lines are redrawn? Elementary and middle-school lines are solved with every open school except Astor and Skyline required to be at 360 (K-5) or 500 (K-8 and 6-8) every year 2027-28 to 2031-32. The walk, past-a-closer-school and move costs are weighted by neighborhood poverty (`weights.equity` 0.5, `build/research/equity/EQUITY_PLAN_SPEC.md`), so the solver prefers to lengthen trips and reassign kids in lower-poverty neighborhoods. High-school lines are PPS Status Quo. The K-5 solve stopped at its 1,800 s limit with a 44% gap (not proven optimal); the 6-8 result is its first round, proven optimal.

**What it shows.** No. Even redrawn from scratch, 33 of 44 elementary schools are at 360 every year 2027-28 to 2031-32 (36 in 2031-32); the rest cannot get there without more closures: in 2031-32 Maplewood is 160 short, Lewis 122, Markham 119, Duniway 97, Sitton 86, Clark 73, Vestal 44, Sunnyside 9 (K-8, against 500). Middle schools: 6 of 14 at 500 in 2031-32 (Lane 382 short, Sellwood 348, George 202, Ockley Green 153). It reassigns 6,708 kids (4,460 K-5, 2,248 6-8) and sends 1,759 K-5 kids past a closer school (as written 2,230). The solve was held to 2027-28 to 2031-32 only: after that, K-5 schools at target fall to 14 in 2032-33 and 2 in 2035-36, because most schools sit just above 360 [inferred: our model on PSU July 2026 Table 5.5]. Read: Plan D's six closures are too few for PPS's 360 bar.

**Older immersion home spread.** Not an issue here: this run finished after the observed Oct 2025 immersion homes were in place, and Plan D moves no immersion strand, so the home file is not used (see `out_v2/planD_redraw_ms/TRADEOFFS.md`).

**Assignment pieces.** Exclave check passes at K-5 and 6-8 (no cut-off pieces); 9-12 is PPS Status Quo (`sq_hs`), whose own pieces are not checked.
