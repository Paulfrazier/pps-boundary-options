How to read this

  • The walk-first lines are one model example, not a proposal. The solver stopped at a feasible plan (31% from its proven bound on K–5), so a better plan may exist. PPS's own tools, with real addresses, could do better still.
  • Same closures as Scenario B: Beach, Buckman, Creston, Irvington, James John, Maplewood, Marysville, Peninsula, Sabin and Woodmere. Immersion programs follow Scenario B's moves. The redraw had to keep at least as many schools at PPS's 360 target each year as B does; it got 30 of 38.
  • Scenario B's lines are our digitized copy of PPS's published B maps. Kid counts are model estimates from Census blocks, not enrollment. Transfers, immersion choices and opt-outs are not modeled. The two plans count slightly different totals ( vs ) because program kids are placed differently.
  • Kids walking past a closer school today (no closures) on the same measure: 1,861. Scenario B raises that; the walk-first lines cut it to about a third of today's.
