Within ½ mile is about a 10-minute walk; 1 mile about 20 minutes. Distances are walking routes on OpenStreetMap from each census block's center, so freeways, rail and the river only count as crossable at real bridges and overpasses. Students are estimated from 2020 Census under-18 counts calibrated to 2024–25 neighborhood enrollment; immersion students choose a program site and are not counted. PPS plan areas are today's areas merged as PPS proposes; where PPS splits a closing school, the line is drawn halfway between the two receiving schools. "Closes" means the school has no neighborhood area in that plan; César Chávez becomes immersion-only in the redraws.

Independent analysis, not an official PPS product. This is a best-effort analysis by a PPS parent and may contain errors. Confirm against PPS's own materials: the Rightsizing scenarios site and the October 6, 2026 Board of Education meeting materials (BoardBook).
