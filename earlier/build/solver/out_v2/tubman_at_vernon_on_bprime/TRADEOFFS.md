"At target" = zoned, non-exempt school between its floor (360 K-5 / 500 K-8 and 6-8 / 1,100 9-12) and its capacity (PPS 2025-26 school profiles), each sized by its own PSU forecast for the year [inferred: our model on PPS/PSU numbers]. The walkable baseline is the earlier v2 solve with no feeder rules and no nesting; its lines were solved for the old 2031-32 → 2040-41 window and are re-scored here.

**What this plan is (request).** PPS Scenario B made sustainable (`bprime_refit_ex`) everywhere, with the Tubman-at-Vernon northeast package from `tubman_at_vernon_beh` in today's Sabin, Irvington, Vernon, King, Alameda, Beverly Cleary and Boise-Eliot/Humboldt areas and the northeast middle schools. King's Mandarin stays at King. Spliced with `build_tubvern_on.py`, not solved. `bprime_refit_ex` is a staging run, not a promoted plan.

**What it shows (2031-32, compared with PPS Scenario B).** Elementary: 35 of 35 counted schools at target every required year vs 16 of 38; 3,871 vs 2,305 moved; 1,558 vs 2,144 past a closer school. Middle school: 13 of 13 vs 7 of 13; average walk 1.61 vs 1.75 mi; 707 vs 1,216 past a closer school; 2,403 vs 2,731 over 1.5 mi.

**Edge cases.** Lent (274) and Rigler (321) are program-only here, below 360 and not counted in the 35. The base's big-school relief cap does not apply in the package's areas, which hold their own lines.
