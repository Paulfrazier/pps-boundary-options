Portland Public Schools rightsizing · Board vote December 15, 2026

Same closures, better lines.

The Superintendent’s recommendation of November 12 leaves 21 of 38 neighborhood elementary schools outside PPS’s own size target in 2031-32, and sends 1,216 K-5 kids more than a mile from home when a school within a mile is open. We modeled other ways to draw the map. Map A is our best overall; map B keeps the recommendation’s closures and fixes the lines.

New, November 15, 2026: Sabin

The recommendation closes Sabin and sends all of it to King. 4 other ways to split Sabin among King, Alameda, Vernon and Beverly Cleary are now on one close-up map, each with school sizes, the walk from every block, and its pros and cons. We don’t pick a winner. The Sabin page shows our smallest fix, the Sabin split, scored on the full model.

Jefferson, year by year

Under the recommendation, Jefferson High School starts at 563 in 2027-28 and doesn’t reach PPS’s 1,100 floor until 2030-31: 563, 804, 979, 1,156, 1,181. PPS’s own 2027-28 projection for Jefferson is 570, close to ours. Map A’s high school lines put it at 885, 1,100, 1,250, 1,399, 1,407. That is short only in the first year, a ramp-up year.

What each one costs

A Map A: our best overall

  • It needs a different school set from the rec: Chávez, Lent and Rigler keep neighborhood areas, and James John, Maplewood and Stephenson close. Those choices, not the lines, explain most of the gap.
  • Feeders are less clean: 53% of middle schools feed one high school (the rec: 81%), and six K-5 zones split across two high schools.
  • 1,317 K-5 kids move out of schools that stay open (337 of them to a longer walk), against 124 under the rec.

B Map B: the rec, lines fixed

  • Keeps the rec’s closures and hubs; one school change, James John into Sitton (as in Scenario A).
  • The Southwest stays short (Maplewood, Forest Park, Stephenson, Markham): six schools have kids for about four. Lines can’t fix that.
  • Middle schools stay at 6 of 14, because it keeps the rec’s middle lines. 1,594 K-5 kids move out of open schools (618 to a longer walk, mostly to fill short schools).

Sources: mapA4/TRADEOFFS.md, mapB/TRADEOFFS.md; moves from the solver’s score_principles.py.

What we’d ask the Board

  1. Adopt the recommendation’s closures, but redraw the lines (map B).
  2. Merge James John into Sitton. Without its Spanish strand, which the rec moves to Chávez, James John is about 233 and Sitton about 208: two small schools side by side.
  3. Revisit the Southwest and the middle school count, which no lines can fill. Map A shows the price of not doing so.

Smallest ask: change only Sabin. Split it by street lines among King, Vernon and Alameda instead of sending all of it to King: Vernon reaches 363, and the worst walks (over 1 mi with a school within ½ mi) drop from 115 to 85. Sabin and NE.
