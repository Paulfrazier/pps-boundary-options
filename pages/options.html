Walking from each closing school's area

Take the neighborhood around a school that PPS closes. Where do its K-5 kids go under each plan, and how far do they walk? Distances are foot-routed block to school; the bus line is 1 mile at K-5.

Each cell: average walk in miles; below it, kids walking more than 1 mile; below that, where the area's kids go (top destinations, kids). K-5 only. "Straight" is straight-line miles to the assigned school. PPS Status Quo keeps the closing school open, so it shows the starting point.
