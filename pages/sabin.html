Sabin and NE

Sabin, King, Vernon and Alameda

The recommendation closes Sabin and sends all of its K-5 kids (about 236 in 2031-32) to MLK Jr. (King). Many of them live closer to Vernon or Alameda. The smallest fix changes only Sabin: split it by street lines.

S Sabin split: the rec, Sabin on street lines

  • Where Sabin’s K-5 kids go (2031-32, our model): 97 west of NE 15th to King; 74 in the northern part of the rest to Vernon; the other 65 to Alameda.
  • Middle and high school: the Alameda piece also goes to Beaumont and Grant, so those kids stay with their Alameda classmates. Everyone else keeps the rec’s lines.
  • Sizes in 2031-32: King 434, Vernon 363 (the rec: 289), Alameda 555, under its 600 ceiling.
  • Walks: K-5 kids past a closer school drop from 2,013 to 1,922. Kids sent over 1 mi with a school within ½ mi: 115 to 85; with a school within 1 mi: 1,216 to 1,123. Kids past a closer school also drop at middle school (1,165 to 1,148) and high school (751 to 701).
  • The costs come from moving the Alameda piece to Beaumont and Grant:
    • Jefferson is 533, 761, 927, 1,094, 1,118 from 2027-28 to 2031-32, 6 short of 1,100 in 2030-31 (the rec: 1,156).
    • Tubman drops under 500 a year earlier (486 in 2028-29).
    • Grant is 1,965 in 2027-28, over its 1,935 seats.
    • Beaumont gains but stays short of 500.

Model v2, run sabin_split (scored, not solved: the rec’s lines with only Sabin changed; scorecard.json, schools.csv). Sabin counts are from the run’s K-5 assignment over today’s Sabin area.

NE NE only: Sabin split plus NE redrawn

  • Sabin splits the same way at K-5 (97 to King, 74 to Vernon, 65 to Alameda), but middle and high school stay as in the rec in every year.
  • The rest of NE’s K-5 lines are redrawn: NE goes from 6 to 10 of 11 schools at 360 (the Sabin split alone: 7). Laurelhurst is still short.
  • Citywide K-5 worst walks (over 1 mi with a school within 1 mi) drop from 1,216 to 1,035; within ½ mi from 115 to 80. Kids past a closer school: 2,013 to 1,843.
  • The cost: 849 K-5 kids move out of NE schools that stay open (402 to a longer walk), to lift short schools and fix the worst walks.

Why not just …

  • …send the south of Sabin to Beverly Cleary? Beverly Cleary sits well to the south. Every line we tried lengthens Sabin’s average walk (0.65 mi to 0.73-0.87 mi) and adds 23 to 56 kids over a mile. Alameda at about 555 is under its ceiling, so it doesn’t need the relief.
  • …draw Vernon’s line farther north? Of the three lines tested, only the northern-part line gets Vernon to 360 (363); the two lines farther north leave it at 350 and 324.
  • …keep Rose City Park open by taking east Alameda? RCP 277, Scott 293, Vernon 312: 16 of 39 schools at 360, worse than the rec. NE has about 4,600 K-5 kids, room for 12 schools at 360, so keeping RCP open leaves one NE school short.
  • …use NE Fremont as the Alameda/Beverly Cleary line? Alameda sits on Fremont: 287 of its kids live south of it, and 220 of those are closer to Alameda.
