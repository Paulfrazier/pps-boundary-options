What each plan changes

The Superintendent’s recommendation, two maps of ours, and an NE-only option. Each runs on the same model, and every school count below is 2031-32.

A Map A: our best overall

Map A is the strongest plan we found. Every school is at target at every level, and the worst walks are nearly gone.

  • School set: ours, carried over from our earlier best plan. Chávez, Lent and Rigler keep neighborhood areas, with immersion alongside; James John, Maplewood and Stephenson close.
  • Lines: K-5 redrawn under the move rule and a worst-walk pass; middle school redrawn for its own K-5 zones; high school redrawn toward one-high-school feeders.
  • Open choice: an earlier version (map A v1, run mapA) has cleaner feeders (60% of middle schools feed one high school, no K-5 zone split across high schools) but longer walks at every level, and high schools short in 2027-28 to 2029-30. This site shows v4.

B Map B: the rec, lines fixed

Map B is the realistic ask: the recommendation’s closures and immersion hubs, plus one change, James John into Sitton, and better K-5 lines.

  • The North works: Sitton about 361, Rosa Parks 408, Astor 460, Chief Joseph 427.
  • NE works: Sabin splits between Vernon and King; Vernon reaches 361.
  • The Southwest can’t be fixed by lines. Map B leaves Forest Park, Maplewood, Markham and Stephenson short and labels them the district’s plan.
  • Middle and high school lines are the rec’s. A re-solve of middle school on map B’s K-5 broke feeders and was rejected. One side effect: 23 of map B’s K-5 zones split across middle schools (the rec: 1).

R The Superintendent’s recommendation

The memo to the Board of November 12, 2026, modeled as written. It closes 10 schools: Beach, Peninsula, Sabin, Irvington, Rose City Park, Buckman, Creston, Lewis, Marysville and Woodmere (the last four in fall 2028). James John, Maplewood, Stephenson and Sellwood Middle School stay open, unlike Scenario A. Mandarin stays at MLK Jr.; Skyline stays with Lincoln.

  • Spanish immersion hubs: Chávez, Rigler, Lent and Ainsworth, immersion-only with no neighborhood area. Their neighborhood kids are sent elsewhere, which is where most of the kids past a closer school come from.
  • Model check: our model gives Stephenson 260 (PPS 261, memo p. 43) and MLK Jr. with all of Sabin 574 (PPS 572 for fall 2027, p. 26).
  • James John: the memo calls it “close to the planning floor of 360” (pp. 25-26) but lists it under 260 (pp. 42-43). The 360 counts its Spanish strand, which the same recommendation moves to Chávez.
  • Timeline: Board presentation Nov 17; listening sessions Nov 19 (Franklin) and Dec 3 (Grant); information session Nov 21 (McDaniel); vote Dec 15.

Source: Superintendent’s recommendation, text (PDF). Our read: super_rec/TRADEOFFS.md.

S Sabin split: the rec, Sabin on street lines

The smallest ask: the recommendation everywhere, except that Sabin is split by street lines instead of going all to King.

  • K-5: west of NE 15th to King; the northern part of the rest to Vernon; the remainder to Alameda (details and the map).
  • Middle and high school: the Alameda piece also goes to Beaumont and Grant, so those kids stay with their Alameda classmates. Everyone else keeps the rec’s middle and high school lines.
  • The costs: Jefferson dips under 1,100 in 2030-31, Tubman drops under 500 a year earlier, and Grant is over its seats in 2027-28.
