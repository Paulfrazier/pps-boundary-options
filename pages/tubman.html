The better plan: move Tubman to where its kids are

These are best effort boundaries. NOT REAL PLANS, yet.

Harriet Tubman Middle School sits at the edge of a freeway and at the edge of the kids it serves. The Vernon building, about 2 miles northeast, fixes both, and PPS has wanted to move Tubman for five years.
