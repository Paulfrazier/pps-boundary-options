Sabin split three ways

The recommendation closes Sabin and sends all of it to Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Our split follows streets: west of NE 15th goes to King, the northern piece to Vernon, and the rest to Alameda, which goes on to Beaumont and Grant. Everything outside Sabin stays the recommendation's. Today's Irvington area is shown too: the recommendation closes Irvington and sends it all to Beverly Cleary, and our split leaves that as is. Where each Sabin block goes

The Superintendent's recommendation loads first. Below it, line options run from the most Sabin sent to Alameda down to the fewest. King always takes Sabin west of NE 15th. Click a row to load it on the map.

School sizes are PPS-anchored: each school starts from PPS's own 2027-28 projection under the recommendation (memo staffing table), grows or shrinks by our model's year-to-year change, and then adds or loses the kids the option moves (our estimate). What the street split does to the schools, by year

Red: under PPS's floor (360 K-5, 500 middle, 1,100 high), or over seats for Grant (1,935). Seats: King 584, Vernon 552, Alameda 771. Only the Alameda piece (65 K-5, 26 middle and 63 high school kids) changes middle and high school; the King and Vernon pieces stay at Tubman and Jefferson, as in the rec.

Sending the Alameda piece to Beaumont and Grant keeps those kids with their Alameda classmates. Beaumont stays over 500 every year. The costs: Grant runs about 33 over its seats in 2027-28, Tubman falls to 500 in 2028-29 (519 under the rec) and under it after, as it does under the rec, and Jefferson only just clears 1,100 in 2030-31 (1,108).
