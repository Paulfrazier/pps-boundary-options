Notes

  • Sizes by year follow each school’s own PSU forecast (July 2026, Table 5.5). These are model estimates, not PPS’s.
  • Map B: the scorecard gives no sizes by year for Forest Park, a Southwest school the plan leaves short (about 259 in 2031-32; about 260 every year under the rec). It counts as short in every year here. Maplewood, Markham and Stephenson keep the rec’s lines, so their years are the rec’s.
  • NE only: the run treats schools outside NE as the district’s plan, and several of them change slightly, so K-5 is shown for 2031-32 only (22 of 38). Middle and high school are the rec’s lines, so their years are the rec’s.
  • Today’s lines include today’s K-8 schools, which use PPS’s K-8 floor of 500 on the whole building.

Model v2 estimates for 2031-32 (years as marked), Census blocks scaled to the PSU forecast. Runs (scorecard.json): mapA4, mapA_sell4, mapB, super_rec, status_quo, sabin_split, ne_plan. Schools at target are neighborhood schools with an attendance area, between PPS’s floor and the building’s capacity; immersion-only hubs and the two small K-8s (Astor, Skyline) aren’t counted. Green marks the best and red the worst of the options (today’s lines aren’t ranked). Worst walks and moves come from the solver’s worst_walks.py and score_principles.py. How we count.
